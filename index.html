The Baltimore Cyber Games

What is the Baltimore Cyber Games?
The Baltimore Cyber Games is an annual competition for Baltimore County public middle school students hosted at Dumbarton Middle School. At the Cyber Games, students learn cybersecurity and technology skills while competing in a fun, beginner-friendly event with friends. They also have the opportunity to meet high school technology clubs and win prizes donated by our sponsors.

When is the Cyber Games?
The 2026 Baltimore Cyber Games took place on March 7th, 2026 at Dumbarton Middle School in Towson, from 12p to 3p. Date and time for the 2027 Baltimore Cyber Games has not been announced yet - please email contact@baltcybergames.com to be placed on our mailing list

Who organizes the Baltimore Cyber Games?
The event is organized by high school students from Eastern Tech, Towson HS, and the Carver Center, and a teacher from Dumbarton Middle School. If you are interested in helping organize the event, send us an email at contact@baltcybergames.com.

Why compete?
Competing lets you explore the exciting world of technology and cybersecurity, meet fellow students interested in tech, gain valuable real-life skills in a fun way, and win awesome prizes. Plus, you get to do it all while working with your friends!
